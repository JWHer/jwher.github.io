Shift vs. Division
In the representation of 𝑛-bit unsigned numbers, performing an 𝑚-bit logical shift or arithmetic shift of a number 𝑥 to the right is equivalent to dividing it by 2 and taking the quotient
3번 쉬프트 = 2^3 = 8로 나눈 것
• 0011101(29) >> 3 = 00000011(3)

However, if 𝑥 < 0, it is different from dividing 𝑥 by 2m and then taking the quotient
*쉬프트가 1로 채워짐
• 11111001(−7) >> 2 = 11111110(−2)

The C99 standard does not define the right shift for negative numbers
• Behavior depends on the implementation of the C compiler
• That is, whether the right shift is a logical shift or an arithmetic shift depends on the compiler implementation
